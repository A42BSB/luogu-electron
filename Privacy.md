# Privacy Policy — luogu-electron

luogu-electron is an unofficial local Electron wrapper for the Luogu web interface.
It does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data from its users.
No telemetry, no analytics, no network calls except those made by the embedded Luogu web pages themselves.

For the privacy terms of the Luogu platform itself, see https://help.luogu.com.cn/ula/luogu
